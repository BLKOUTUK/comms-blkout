Making Ourselves From Scratch — Croydon Loves You 2026
Your face here.
Black queer men rarely see ourselves on a magazine cover. This autumn, eight of us in Croydon get to make one.
Making Ourselves From Scratch is three workshops at the Waterside Centre in South Norwood, late October into November. Choose your place — a Croydon corner that carries our history. Find your headline, with writer and performer Derek Aidoo. Make the cover — life-size, built together, with you on it. Then host your people: dinner at Stanley Arts on Joseph Beam Day, 28 December.
It is also the first time we've said the name out loud: BLKOUT Review, the magazine of the Black Queer Men's Liberation Collective. You can be on its cover before there's an issue. No experience of writing, performing or being photographed needed — just a Croydon connection and something to say.

Black History Month — #BLKOUTAreYouIn
We come together. Are you in?
We come together, to make space, to create connections that build power. That's October: four weeks, four beats, told from our own archive — ten years of making, in colour, by the people who were there. Not a history lesson. The receipts for an invitation.
