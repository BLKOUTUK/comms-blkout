The BLKOUT picnic – Regent's Park, August 2026
You're invited – our first AGM
Come and shape membership.
On Wednesday 25 November at 7pm, BLKOUT holds its first annual general meeting – online, and open to you. A year ago twelve of us registered a co-operative owned by the people it serves. On Joseph Beam Day, 28 December, membership opens to everyone, and before it does we want to hear from you: what should joining BLKOUT mean, and what do you want from it?
You don't need to be a member to come. The formal business takes about twenty minutes – the accounts, electing the board and one decision about our audit – and members vote on it. The rest of the evening is an open conversation about membership, and everyone is welcome to take part.
Want to see what we did with the year first? Our directors have written about it, in their own words, in BLKOUT's Year One.

BLKOUT Voices – What We Pass Along
Five short films, five Mondays.
From 16 November, a new short film every Monday until 14 December: Black queer men, in their own voices, on what we inherit and what we choose to hand on. Love as practice opens the series and Passing it on closes it, with three more in between.
