From the Brother to Brother campaign
Worn close to the heart — Brother to Brother, redesigned
In 1991, Black gay men made a book to hold each other. Brother to Brother — begun by Joseph Beam, finished by Essex Hemphill after Beam's death — put our tenderness in print and dared the world to look. Thirty-five years on, we've put its cover back where it belongs: over the heart.
The new tees carry the two brothers as a small badge. Ten centimetres of recognition. Some brothers will clock it across the room — that's the point. The classic big-print tees and hoods stay too, loud as ever. From £19, and every sale feeds a pot our community owns.
The timing is deliberate. It's payday — and the post is free: free UK delivery on everything, this weekend only, ends Sunday midnight. If a shirt has been sitting on your list, this is the weekend it costs nothing extra to arrive.
