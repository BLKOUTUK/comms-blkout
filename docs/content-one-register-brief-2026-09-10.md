## The shape (decided)
One register (`content_calendar`) → one read surface (`/admin/calendar` → "Content") → one posting workflow (`/post`, writes back) → approvals land in the register. Vocabulary for `status`: **draft · ready · scheduled · posted · skipped**. `metadata` carries: `campaign` (slug), `channels` (["instagram","facebook","linkedin"]), `source` (`file:<name>#<item id>` | `agent:<task id>` | `manual`), `posted` ({channel: {id, url, at}}), `legacy` (anything else from the import, incl. Canva design ids — never used). `media_urls` = public https URLs only.
